I'm Hatem, a web developer and security engineer based in Giza, Egypt.

My day job is digital forensics and incident response at EG-CERT, Egypt's national Computer Emergency Response Team. I've worked on more than 40 investigations there, working out how attackers got in, what they touched, and how to stop it happening again.

That shapes how I build for clients. HomeFix and Baseline were designed with the attacker in mind from the first line: row-level security on every table, rate limits, private file storage, and automated tests that try to break in before anyone else can.

I care just as much about the other side: interfaces that are fast and clear, that work in English and Arabic, and that feel right on a phone in someone's hand.
