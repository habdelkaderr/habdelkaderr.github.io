Hi, I'm Hatem. I build websites and web apps from Giza, Egypt.

By day I work at EG-CERT, Egypt's national cybersecurity team, where I've worked on more than 40 investigations: how the attacker got in, what they touched, and how to make sure it doesn't happen again.

That rubs off on everything I build. Security isn't something I add at the end; it's there from the start. HomeFix and Baseline were both built that way, and both are tested by software that tries to break in before anyone else can.

But a secure site that nobody enjoys using isn't much use. So I care just as much about the part you see: fast pages, clear buttons, English and Arabic, and screens that feel natural on a phone.
