HomeFix

A home-repair service needed to turn WhatsApp community posts into booked, tracked jobs, without paying for software.

Problem
Requests arrived as scattered chat messages. There was no record of who to call back, which technician was going, or which posts brought customers in.
Solution
A one-minute, no-account request form (English and Arabic) feeding a private dashboard: contact via WhatsApp, schedule, assign a technician, quote, get paid, collect a rating.
Outcome
Every request is traceable from the original group post to revenue, and the whole system costs $0/month to run.
  • 3-step request flow with photo upload and automatic image compression
  • Admin dashboard: today's work list, appointments calendar, spare-parts tracking, analytics
  • One-tap WhatsApp messages, pre-written in the customer's language
  • Separate technician portal: each technician sees only their own jobs
  • Phone push notifications when a customer's chosen contact time starts
  • Campaign links showing which community group brings in paying jobs
  • Staff vs. owner roles, plus a one-click Excel backup

Built to be trusted

Row-level security on every table, rate limiting, spam honeypot, private photo storage with expiring links, strict security headers. Covered by 437 automated checks: security, full browser workflows, and layouts at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

  • Vite
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Web Push

Installable web app (PWA) · Works offline · Privacy-first

Baseline

A training app that decides what you should do today, based on how recovered you are and what you've actually done. All your data stays on your own phone.

Problem
Fixed workout plans ignore a bad night's sleep or yesterday's football match, and most fitness apps upload your health data to someone else's server.
Solution
A one-minute daily check-in produces one recommended session, with its reasoning shown. Any wearable works, or none. No account and no server.
Outcome
It installs to the home screen like a native app, works fully offline, and ships with zero third-party libraries.
  • Adaptive engine: 173 exercises, 18 sports, filtered by the equipment you actually own
  • "Why this?" explains every recommendation, including what was ruled out
  • Hands-free workout screen: plus/minus buttons, auto-starting rest timer, last session's numbers
  • Imports from WHOOP, Garmin, Oura, Fitbit, Apple Watch and more, with upload validation
  • Injury-aware: works around affected movements until the date you set
  • Data export and full erase, plus a written privacy policy that matches the code
  • Light and dark themes
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • HTML/CSS
  • IndexedDB
  • Service Worker
  • PWA
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Node build
