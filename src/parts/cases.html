Web app for a home-repair business

HomeFix

A repair business was drowning in WhatsApp messages. HomeFix turned them into booked jobs, for $0 a month.

The problem
Job requests were scattered across group chats. Nobody could tell who needed a callback, which technician was going, or which posts actually brought in customers.
What I built
A one-minute request form in English or Arabic, with no sign-up. Behind it, a private dashboard where the owner messages customers on WhatsApp, books visits, sends a technician, quotes, gets paid and collects a rating.
The result
Every job can be traced back to the post that brought it in, and the whole system runs for $0/month.
  • A three-step request form with photo upload
  • A daily to-do list, calendar, spare-parts tracker and simple reports
  • One-tap WhatsApp replies, already written in the customer's language
  • A separate view for technicians, showing only their own jobs
  • Phone reminders when it's time to call a customer back
  • Links that show which WhatsApp group brings in paying customers
  • Owner and staff accounts, plus a one-click Excel backup

Locked up tight

Customer data is private by default, spam bots get turned away, and photos are stored securely. Before anything goes live, 437 automated checks try to break it on phones, tablets and computers.

  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Web Push
  • Playwright

Fitness app that works offline

Baseline

A workout app that asks how you slept before it tells you what to do today. Your data never leaves your phone.

The problem
Most workout plans are written weeks ahead and don't care that you slept badly or played football yesterday. And most fitness apps send your health data to their servers.
What I built
A one-minute check-in each morning, then one workout picked for how you feel today, with a plain explanation of why. It works with a fitness tracker or without one, and there's no account to create.
The result
It installs on your phone like a normal app, works without internet, and keeps everything on your device.
  • 173 exercises and 18 sports, matched to the equipment you actually have
  • A "Why this?" button that explains every suggestion
  • A workout screen you can use mid-set: big buttons and a rest timer that starts on its own
  • Works with WHOOP, Garmin, Oura, Fitbit, Apple Watch and more
  • Works around injuries until you're ready
  • Export or delete all your data whenever you like
  • Light and dark mode
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • HTML/CSS
  • IndexedDB
  • Service Worker
  • PWA
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Node build
