AI Command Center

One place for tasks, notes, documents and projects, with an AI assistant that can actually get things done, but never without asking.

It runs on your own computer. The assistant can find, create and update your tasks, notes and files, while anything risky, like deleting or overwriting, waits for your approval. Every change can be undone, and without AI everything else still works offline.

PC Health Dashboard

Task Manager's smarter cousin. It shows what your PC is doing, explains why it's slow, and never guesses.

Live charts for CPU, memory, graphics, disks and network, plus temperatures, startup apps and a diagnostics page that explains each problem in plain words with what to do next. If a reading isn't available, it says so instead of making one up. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing changes without your confirmation.

  • Floating desktop widgets that stay on top while you work
  • Turn startup apps off safely, with one-click undo
  • Find what's filling your drive, including duplicate files
  • Works without administrator rights
